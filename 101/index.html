Bottom-Up Curriculum Philosophy

Legal Engineering 101 courseware is designed strictly from the bottom up—crystallizing scaled, repeatable patterns as they organically emerge from weekly Socratic Events, Google Hangout calls, and member discussions. It does not matter that quiz questions start locked; as scaled patterns emerge from real-world clinic sessions, courses are formalized into structured, verifiable assessment modules.
