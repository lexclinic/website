📖 Class 3.1 Key Concepts & Takeaways

📜 1. The Legal Genesis of Markup (GML)

In 1969, Charles Goldfarb, Edward Mosher, and Raymond Lorie developed GML at IBM specifically to manage legal documents, formatting, and structural tag decomposition.

🌐 2. From Legal Tagging to HTML & the Web

GML evolved into SGML (ISO 8879) and directly laid the structural foundation for HTML and XML—meaning the modern World Wide Web grew directly out of legal document tagging!
