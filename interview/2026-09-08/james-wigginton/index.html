James Wigginton is a member of Orrick's Technology Companies Group and Blockchain & Digital Assets practice, based in Sacramento and Silicon Valley. He advises entrepreneurs and investors pioneering novel operating models across finance, social media, Web3, gaming, and emerging technology.
