ℹ️ How LexClinic ERC-7827 Attestations Work

1. Complete Assessments

Take Socratic quizzes across Legal Engineering 101 classes, weekly live sessions, and roadmap interviews while logged in with your email.

2. Keccak-256 State Hashing

Each submission generates a deterministic Keccak-256 payload hash of your answers, score, email, and timestamp stored in the LexClinic Student Store.

3. On-Chain Counter-Signing

Once the ERC-7827 attestation contract is deployed, LexClinic will counter-sign your recorded payload hashes on-chain, transitioning your status from "Yet Attested To" to "Attested On-Chain".
