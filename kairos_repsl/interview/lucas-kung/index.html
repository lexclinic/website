👤 Speaker & Interviewer Profile

🇨🇭

Lukas Küng

DAO Researcher • ZHAW / UNIC / DAWO Organizing Chair

Lukas Küng is a prominent Swiss academic researcher specializing in Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) business value and governance at the ZHAW School of Management and Law and the University of Nicosia (IFF). He serves as a core organizing committee member and track chair for the European DAO Workshop (DAWO24, DAWO25 in Zurich, DAWO26 in Winterthur) and is co-author of Mastering DAOs: A Practical Guidebook.

Interview Overview & Core Themes

In this deep-dive academic dialogue, Lukas Küng and Kyle MacLean Smith explore how DAOs can escape general partnership joint-liability traps, why paper contracts are obsolete specification models, and how smart legal wrappers code human accountability directly into smart contract verification gates.

🏛️ Surviving Corporate Law

Unwrapped DAOs default to general partnerships with joint and several liability. Legal wrappers establish limited liability boundaries for contributors.
