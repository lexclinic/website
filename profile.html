ℹ️ LexClinic Membership & Governance Rules

LexClinic operates under a dual-entity fiduciary structure:

  • LexClinic Education, Inc. (Charity): Delaware non-profit public benefit corporation on its way to formal 501(c)(3) status. Operates open courseware and receives charitable contributions.
  • LexClinic Community LLC (Working Entity): The working member entity where contributors, researchers, and legal engineers do active work.
  • Official Identity: Becoming a working LLC member includes receiving an official @lex.clinic email address, unlocking private governance bylaws and LLCA records.
