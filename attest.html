What is "Yet Attested To"? (ERC-7827 On-Chain Counter-Party Attestation)

When you complete a quiz at LexClinic Education, your answers are hashed on the client side using Keccak-256 to produce a unique, deterministic state payload hash (e.g. 0xa3f2...8b9c) which is recorded in the LexClinic Student Store.

🏛️ Pending On-Chain Counter-Party Attestation

Formal ERC-7827 EVM personal ledger counter-party attestations allow LexClinic Education, Inc. to counter-sign your Keccak-256 quiz state hashes as an official institutional issuer.

Because the ERC-7827 EVM smart contract specification is currently in pre-deployment, your scores are securely recorded client-side and in the student store, marked as "Yet Attested To". Once the ERC-7827 protocol goes live on-chain, LexClinic Education, Inc. will issue institutional counter-party attestation signatures directly into your personal ERC-7827 state machine.
